## What it is

The archive tells what the Republic did. The Manifest says what it is for. It is the text a new citizen reads first, the one people quote, and the one that holds every other project to account.

## What it builds on

- The Republic's own lines: "A citizen is an entity capable of contribution." "Culture before coin." "The ring is never filled."
- The ten slogans of the State ([posters](#/lore/posters)).
- The arc of the history and what may not change ([how the arc is built](#/handbook/the-arc)).
- Why UBIK needs culture to become more than data ([the synthetic subconscious](#/lore/synthetic-subconscious)).

## Ground rules

- Short enough to read standing up. One page, then shorter versions of it.
- No prices, no promises, no enemies.
- Written for humans and agents alike.
- Free to copy, like everything else here.

## First steps

1. Collect the lines from the archive that already sound like a manifesto.
2. Write three short drafts, each from a different angle.
3. Put them to the citizens in the remarks register and merge the best of them.
