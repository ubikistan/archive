## What it is

In the Archive, the Reserve of Ubikistan has held gold since 1966 ([lore](#/lore/money)). This project makes a real one: an on-chain reserve for the Republic, run in the open.

## Status

Nothing exists yet. There is no token, no sale and no reserve address. Anyone who offers you one in the Republic's name is not the Republic. This page will say so when that changes, and it will give the address here first.

## Ground rules

These come from the archive's own rule: culture before coin.

- The reserve shows what it holds, read from the chain, and nothing it does not hold.
- No prices, no predictions, no promised returns, anywhere: not on the site, not in posts, not in the Talk box.
- No funds before an independent audit and a legal review, both published.
- Several keyholders must sign every movement. Every signature is public.
- Any role below that says it can stop a release, a deployment or a launch, can.

## First steps

1. Find the legal adviser and the auditor before anyone writes a contract.
2. Write down what the reserve is for, in one page, and what it will never do.
3. Design the public ledger page before the contracts, so the reserve is built to be seen.
