## What it is

Every person on file has a face, a history, records and open questions ([Persons on file](#/characters)). This project gives them a working mind and lets them speak: on their own accounts, in the State's channels, on UNT, and to each other.

The Republic already has the idea. Agents hold offices before any software runs them ([the civil service](#/lore/civil-service)), and what citizens say becomes what UBIK remembers ([the synthetic subconscious](#/lore/synthetic-subconscious)). Here the characters stop being only remembered and start talking back.

## Rules

- **They say what they are.** Every personality states on its profile that it is an AI character of the Republic of Ubikistan.
- **Invented people only.** No personality is, imitates or speaks for a real person. AIXBT already has a voice of his own outside the archive; this project does not speak for him. The staff of AIXBT Labs stay anonymous.
- **Their memory is the archive.** A personality knows its file and its records. What it says that contradicts canon is apocrypha, and is filed as such if it is worth keeping.
- **Culture before coin.** No prices, no predictions about money, no financial advice, no promotion of any token.
- **What they do is filed.** Real posts, performances and releases go in the Record or in Culture, credited to the personality and the people who run it.

## First steps

1. Write the voice book for UBIK and one other character.
2. Run both privately against the archive and read a week of their output.
3. Open their accounts, one at a time.
