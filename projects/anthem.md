## What it is

The Republic has had a television channel since 1968 and national holidays since the founding, and no anthem in the Archive. This project writes one, and the smaller pieces of State sound around it.

## What it builds on

- UNT channel 1, its test card and its idents ([Ubikistan National Television](#/lore/unt)).
- The calendar of national holidays, where an anthem would be played ([holidays](#/lore/holidays)).
- The ring, which is never filled ([the ring](#/lore/symbols)).

## Ground rules

- Original music only. No borrowed melodies, no samples of anyone else's work.
- Released free to copy, like the archive, so anyone can play it.
- No real people's voices imitated, and no voice cloning of anyone who has not agreed to it.

## First steps

1. Decide whether the anthem has words.
2. Collect sketches: thirty seconds each, any instrument.
3. Choose one with the citizens, then make the full version and the ident.
