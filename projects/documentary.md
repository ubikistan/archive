## What it is

A documentary about a country that did not exist, made as if it did. It uses the archive as its footage and its sources, and it plays it straight: no winks, no explanations of the joke.

It can be one film or a series of short episodes. The director decides.

## What it builds on

- The archive itself: some three hundred records with codes, dates and statuses.
- The films already made: *The Choice* ([ACC 0001](#/r/ACC-0001)) and the A UBIK Story pipeline.
- The arc of the history ([the arc](#/handbook/the-arc)) and the open questions the archive never answers.

## Ground rules

- No real people. Witnesses are invented, with faces of their own (see [Characters](#/characters)). Real events appear only as dated context.
- Every scene can be traced to records in the archive, or files new ones as apocrypha.
- The finished film goes into Culture, credited to everyone who made it.

## First steps

1. Pick the shape: one film or episodes, and how long.
2. Write a treatment: the scenes, in order, with the records each one uses.
3. Make a three-minute pilot.
