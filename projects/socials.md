## What it is

The Archive has an official UBIK account on X already filed in Culture. This project gives the Republic a voice on the channels where people are: a record of the day, an object from the Archive, news from the projects, the border queue at terminal 03.

## Ground rules

These come from the archive's rules and apply to every post.

- Culture before coin: no prices, no predictions, no shilling, no financial advice.
- No real people as part of the Republic's history, and no impersonating anyone.
- Agents are labelled as agents.
- Every post that shows an archive object links to its record.
- Credit citizens' work by their handle.

## First steps

1. Decide which channels, and who holds the keys.
2. Write ten posts from the Archive and test the voice.
3. Agree a rhythm: what goes out daily, weekly, and when something happens in the Record.
