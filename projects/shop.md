## What it is

Objects from the Republic, made properly. Each product is something the archive already holds: the Tour of the Plain jersey, the patches, the ring pin, the stamp sheets, the passport cover. Each ships with its archive code and a printed card saying which record it comes from.

It lives on the Ubikistan website ([website project](#/projects/website-id)).

## Ground rules

- Few things, made well. A product earns its place the way a record does.
- Retail quality or nothing: real materials, real makers, samples before stock.
- The ring is the symbol. No real brands, no real people, no aerosol cans.
- The shop sells objects. It does not sell tokens or anything that pretends to be one.

## First steps

1. Choose the first five objects from the archive.
2. Find makers and order samples.
3. Photograph the samples and decide what goes on sale.
