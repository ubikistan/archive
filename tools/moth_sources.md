# Material MOTH reads beside the archive

MOTH (tools/moth.py) reads this file on every run, after the archive itself. It lists what is being built around the
archive, so the finds can bridge to it. Add to it when something new is made. Credit stays with the makers.

## The Field Guide (the present-day Republic, by rel_op)

The team's Field Guide is the Republic as it looks today: a small state on a raised plateau, about 20 metres above the
plain, with a hinterland around it. It looks 1960s and 1970s and has never been torn down; new things are fitted
inside old buildings. It is always day. Its inhabitants have their own look and uniforms (officials, lab technicians,
technicians, border guards). A 3D model of it is being built as stages for stories.

The rule between the two: anything visible in the present follows the Field Guide; the past, and what goes on inside
the buildings, belongs to the Archive. So a find may say where an object was kept, made or found using these places,
and may show an interior changing while the outside stays the same. Never move an institution the archive has already
placed, never describe a new building after the 1970s (later institutions are refits inside old buildings), and never
describe the outside of a place in a way that fights its name.

Places, and the institutions the proposed mapping puts in them:
- The Assembly in the Crater: Ministry of State Affairs (MSA); the State Archive (SA) on its lower floors
- The core round the Center: the 1968 ministry complex round an empty square
- The Quiet Pavilions: UBIK Systems (UBK) and the Ministry for Machine Affairs (MMA); 1990s machines in 1960s pavilions
- The Sanatorium: the Institute for Applied Consciousness, Station 6 (IAC)
- The Counting House, with its vault: the Reserve (RES); the vault stays mostly empty; gold from the gold works in the hinterland
- The Relay Line: the State Post (PTT), and later the network's control room (1998)
- The Listening Array: AIXBT Labs (AXL)
- The UNT television centre: Ubikistan National Television (UNT) and the Ministry of Culture (MCA)
- The Border Gate (guard hut, inspection office, barrier): the Border Service (BSV); the Register of Synthetic Persons (RSP) in the inspection office
- The Cooling Lakes: the National Institute for Computation and Communications (NICC), the State's computing centre
- The Motor Yard and the overspill, the dirt tracks of the Tour of the Plain: the Sporting Committee (SPC)
- The Long Baseline: the Orbital Programme (UOP), the 1979 dish and bunker
- The Secret Service (SEC) has no building; the Ministry has never confirmed it exists
- Open ground, with no institution yet: the Ring Estate (housing), the Weather Ridge, the Glasshouses, the Heat Wells,
  and the hinterland apart from the gold works. Ordinary life lives here.

## The Open Centre (by Ellis Liu)

A community broadcast of the Republic, "a country that may or may not exist", at ubikistan.ellisliu.art. It treats
Ubikistan as something that is on air. A find may mention a broadcast, a schedule or a transmission as the Republic's
own, never the Open Centre by name.

## The forecast desk (UBIK's forecasts at ubik.gold)

UBIK issues forecasts on world events every day and is scored when they settle. MOTH gets the open questions and the
recently settled ones in its gap report. They are the outside world as the Republic watches it. Never a figure, never
the word prediction, odds or bet.
