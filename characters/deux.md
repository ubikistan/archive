A Paris duo who met UBIK in 1997 as two young men and stopped appearing in public without black face shields in 2000: a smooth shield, a single red slit, ivory neck plates, formal workwear, no visible skin. From 2003 the Border Service listed them as one entity of class COLLECTIVE. Their retrospective in 2013 was called HUMAN AFTER IDENTITY.
