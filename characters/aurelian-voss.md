Born in Trieste in 1949, raised in Geneva, trained as an actuary. In reinsurance until 1988, where he was known as the only man in the room who had priced death correctly. One of the four founding partners of Darkstone Enterprises; chairman and chief executive since 1992, and the only partner who stayed.

He appears only on screens, from the same grey room, in the same grey suit, under a single lamp. He is calm and courteous and has never been recorded raising his voice or making a threat. He asks for things. He refers to companies that have merged and a prime minister who is dead. His sentences are a fraction longer each year.

No relation to Mara Voss ([FIG 0102](#/characters)). The Border Service checked in 1994 and again in 2019.

CENSOR-0 keeps a working hypothesis about him in file DS-0001, classified unconfirmed. The Archive holds what can be seen: the footage, the lamp, the Letters.
