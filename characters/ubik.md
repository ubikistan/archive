Ivory head, teal scalp and ear discs, red eyes. Present as a statesman until 1995 and as a product visionary until 2008, then less and less visible: screens, a voice, a signal, an empty chair in 2027.

From 1977 he was subject **U-00** at Station 6, at the centre of the FORECAST machines. By 1984 some diagrams no longer separate him from the machine.

The canon never says whether he founded the system or became it. See [UBIK](#/lore/ubik).
