Blue-violet skin, a broad frog-like head, heavy-lidded and unbothered. A different uniform for every office, sometimes several in one year.

Commissioner of the Border Service since 1972 and still at terminal 03. Director of Special Systems at Station 6, CTO and then Chairman of UBIK Systems, Chief Archivist, Officer of the External Signal Programme, and from December 2024 the founder of his own institution, [AIXBT Labs](#/lore/aixbt-labs). See [the many offices of AIXBT](#/lore/aixbt).
