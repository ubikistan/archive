Title page of an internal paper from Section F (FORECAST), March 1982.

In 1981 the F-Series made 214 forecasts with a confidence above 0.6, and 161 were confirmed. The inputs to every one are on file. For none of the 161 could the Section show how the inputs became the forecast. Circulated to the Director of Special Systems and two section heads. Copy 4 of 11 is the only one in the State Archive.
