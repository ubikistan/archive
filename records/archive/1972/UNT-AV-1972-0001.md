A UNT tape box labelled for the signing at Sector 03, 1972, holding a reel of line-up tone.

Grey board box for a 7 in. reel. Typed label on the lid: "UNT · OUTSIDE SOUND · SECTOR 03 · SIGNING · 1972 · REEL 1 OF 1 · 7½ IPS". The spine repeats "SECTOR 03" and nothing else. No day or month is given anywhere on the box. The dubbing card inside the lid has three columns, DATE · DUBBED TO · BY, and all three are empty.

In pencil on the inside of the lid: "Camera not admitted at barrier. Sound only." Beneath it, in another hand: "Hold for Ministry." Neither note is initialled. The Border Service was founded the same year, and its barrier at Sector 03 is in BSV/PH/1972/0007.

The reel in the box carries its own leader label, in a third hand: "LINE-UP TONE". Played once at accession, it holds a steady tone for about thirty-one minutes and then runs silent to the end. Whether this is the reel that went to Sector 03 is not recorded. UNT's tape store has no other box labelled Sector 03.

Found on the bottom shelf of the sound store at the UNT television centre, behind the weather binders, with its lid taped down.
