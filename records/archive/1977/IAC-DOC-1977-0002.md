Log of a closed-loop trial with subject U-00, 21:04 on 5 September to 08:47 on 6 September 1977: 11 hours 43 minutes.

The last line before LOG END has no operator code: VALVE 7 WILL FAIL AT 14:22. Valve 7, on cooling line B, failed at 14:22 on 7 September. The note in red was added after the failure. The log also records an observer at 07:30, 'A. (DIR SS)'.

Disputed: the paper is genuine, but nothing shows whether the line was printed before the failure or added after it.
