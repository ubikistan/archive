Identification sheet for subject U-00, entered March 1977. Name, date of birth, place of origin, species, height and next of kin are blank.

The photograph was held by the Director of Special Systems and is not with the form. Series U is not defined on any Institute key; someone has circled it in red and asked. Coupling tolerance reads NO LIMIT REACHED. The countersignature is illegible. The State Archive identifies U-00 as UBIK and does not say more.
