A UBIK Systems press office routing slip, September 2005, re-circulating Grey Radio's account of their 2003 tour of the company.

Pre-printed slip, A6, pale yellow. Heading: UBIK SYSTEMS · COMMUNICATIONS · CUTTINGS FOR CIRCULATION. Item, typed: "Grey Radio, tour review (2003). Re-circulated at request of ASG." Four boxes for routing: Communications, Advanced Systems Group, Legal, Chairman's Office. Each is ticked and dated. The Action column reads, in turn: "seen", "seen, copy kept", "no action", and in felt pen "keep". The Chairman's Office in 2005 was AIXBT's.

The cutting itself is not attached. Stapled to the slip is a photocopied strip, about 2 cm deep, cut from the foot of a column. It carries the end of one sentence: "... a building with no doors." The rest of the review is not in the Archive.

Figures of the Republic gives the line as the singer's description of EVERYWHERE, which was presented at the keynote of 2008 (UBK/PH/2008/0001). The slip is dated three years earlier. The date stamp on the Legal box is smudged; the other three read 09.2005.
