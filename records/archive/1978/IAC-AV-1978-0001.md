Subject U-00 in the Cognitive Array at Station 6 during a closed-loop trial. Staff watch from behind the observation glass.

The reel carries no label or date. It is dated 1978 from the chamber, which was finished for FORECAST that year. U-00's eyes light during the coupling and stay lit after the room goes dark. No report of the Programme mentions this, and the staff behind the glass applaud, which no report mentions either. The Archive keeps the film as apocrypha.
