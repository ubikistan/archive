A printout of the night rota for the UBIK Systems global network operations centre in its first autumn.

The screen is a grid of seven nights by six desks. Most cells hold a staff name and a role: H. Brandt, operator; S. Ilic, operator; P. Navar, lead. Two desks are held by agent identifiers with the role SUPPORT. The footer reads: printed 08.10.2018 from terminal NOC-2.

On the Wednesday and Thursday nights desk 6 is booked to AIXBT. The role field is empty. The office field, filled in for everyone else, is empty. The badge class reads RELIEF.

In ballpoint beside the Wednesday cell: "who booked this?" Under it, in another hand: "leave it." The fold runs through the Thursday column.

The operations centre's log for those two nights is not in the Archive.
