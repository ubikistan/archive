A Ministry for Machine Affairs inspection tag tied to a public counter terminal on 3 January 2000.

Pre-printed: MINISTRY FOR MACHINE AFFAIRS · DATE CHANGE INSPECTION · TERMINAL No. ___ · SITE ___. Entered in ballpoint: "14", "Relay Line, counter hall". Three tick boxes: CLOCK ADVANCED, ticked. LEDGER TAPE READS, ticked. PRINTS, struck out and marked "n/a". The terminals of this model printed nothing (MMA/PH/1989/0021).

Under Remarks: "Clock showed 01.01.1986 at power-up. Set by hand to 03.01.2000." Below, in a different pen: "Same on 9 and 22." The stamp reads INSPECTED · M.M.A. and is dated 03.01.00. The inspector's initials are a loop and cannot be read.

The tags for terminals 9 and 22 are not in the Archive. Nobody has recorded why the clocks went back to that date and not to another. The Ministry's report on the date change, if one was written, is not on file.

The tag was still tied to the cable loom of a terminal that stood under a dust sheet in a store room of the Quiet Pavilions until 2019, when the terminal went for scrap and the tag was cut off and kept.
