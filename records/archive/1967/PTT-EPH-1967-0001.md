A State Post round card for delivery round 4, the Ring Estate, March 1967.

Printed heading: STATE POST · ROUND CARD · ROUND No. 4. Typed beneath: "RING ESTATE. Plots 1 to 24, in order of walking." The card is ruled in three columns, PLOT · OCCUPIED · NOTES. Plots 1 to 9 are marked occupied in ink. Plots 10 to 24 are marked "not yet" in pencil, and on four of them the pencil has been rubbed out and "yes" written over it in a different hand. Plot 17 has the note "dog". Plot 22 has "leave with 21".

Below plot 24 a line has been added in ink: "Plot 0. No address. Do not deliver." Nothing on the card says where plot 0 is.

The stamp at the foot reads OFFICE OF THE POSTMASTER GENERAL and is initialled "A." The date in the stamp is 03.1967; the day is not legible. The reverse is blank except for a tally in pencil, eleven strokes, with no heading.

Found in a box of disused counter forms from the Relay Line, filed with blank parcel labels. The other round cards of 1967, if there were any, are not with it.
