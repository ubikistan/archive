The first of Darkstone Enterprises' annual Letters, addressed to every entity in which it held a position. The State Archive's copy carries CENSOR-0's marginal notes in red.

It opens: "We have been thinking about time." It asks, on the third page, that each recipient publish a five-year plan. Most did. The margin reads: *first small thing*.

The Republic was not a recipient. The copy came through the Ministry of State Affairs from a foreign pension fund that was. No later Letter mentions the Republic either; the file counts the absences.
