A trading hall in January 1994 during Darkstone's first broadcast address, the same picture on every monitor in the wall. Aurelian Voss, then 45, fingers steepled behind a black lectern. The earliest footage of him in the State Archive's file; the black room he later speaks from does not yet exist.
