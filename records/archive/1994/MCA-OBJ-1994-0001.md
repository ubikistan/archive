An ivory stage earpiece deposited with the Ministry of Culture by Inés Calder in November 1994, engraved inside with a UBIK Systems prototype mark.

The shell is matte ivory resin, 38 mm long, shaped to sit in the right ear. A small red ring, painted, sits on the outer face. The cable is grey, cut cleanly at 40 cm, with no plug. The box is grey card with a lift-off lid. The label on the lid is typed: "DEPOSIT. Earpiece, stage, 1. Depositor: I. Calder. For exhibition VOICE AND MACHINE. Received 11.1994." A second line has been added in ballpoint: "Exhibition not held. Retain until collected."

Inside the shell, engraved small enough to need a lens: "A.S.G. PROTO 3". The Advanced Systems Group of UBIK Systems is recorded as set up in 1995 (UBK/PH/1995/0001, in its first year). The box was received the year before. Figures of the Republic says the earpiece was of Calder's own design.

The box was on a shelf of the Ministry of Culture's store at the UNT television centre, with no catalogue of the exhibition and no correspondence. It was never collected. Prototypes 1 and 2 are not in the Archive.
