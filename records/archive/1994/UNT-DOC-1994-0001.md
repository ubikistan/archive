A page of the UNT music desk's register of material sent in unasked, with the entry for a cassette signed Spool.

Columns: NO. · RECEIVED · ITEM · SENDER · ACTION. Entry 94/31, received 09.94: "1 audio cassette, no label, in UBIK Systems padded mailer." Sender: "none given. SPOOL written on cassette in marker." Action: "Played. Not broadcast."

The action column continues in other hands. "Returned to UBIK Systems 10.94." Then: "Back again. UBK say not theirs. Returned 11.94." Then, in pencil: "Retained." The entries above and below are ordinary: a choir from the south district, a tape of birdsong, a request for a test card.

The cassette is not in the UNT archive and is not in the State Archive. The mailer is not described beyond its maker. The register was transferred to the State Archive in 2008 with the UNT paper files, which survived the move to the network because nobody had copied them.
