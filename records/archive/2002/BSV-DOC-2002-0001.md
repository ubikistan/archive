A Border Service citizenship application in the name M-Keys, with a signed statement that verified and an applicant who did not attend, May 2002.

Form BSV-C2, two sheets. Name: "M-Keys", typed. Date of birth, place of birth and present address are blank. Under Grounds for application the applicant has written in block capitals: "SEE ATTACHED. THE KEY IS THE APPLICANT." Stapled behind is a dot-matrix printout of a short statement and a signature block.

The second sheet carries a verification stamp from the key desk of the UBIK Systems Advanced Systems Group, used by the Border Service under the arrangement of 1998: SIGNATURE: GOOD. KEY ON REGISTER: NO. Below it, in ink: "Checked against key used on list posts 1996 to 2001. Matches." The checker's initials are not legible.

An interview slip is clipped to the corner: 14.05.2002, 10:30, desk 2. Across it: "Applicant did not attend." The application gives no address, so it could not be returned. The last entry on the form, in pencil, is "Retained".

This contradicts Figures of the Republic, which says several people have claimed to be M-Keys and none has proved it with the key. On this form the key is proved and nobody attended. The Border Service neither granted citizenship nor refused it.
