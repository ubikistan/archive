Cover of the first subject register of Project INTERFACE, Institute for Applied Consciousness, Station 6.

The label lists four subject series: H (human), C (primate), D (canine) and A (avian). A fifth series, U, appears inside from 1977 and is not on the cover. The binder came to the State Archive under seal in 1987 and was opened in part when it was declassified.
