A flat on the Ring Estate in April 2012, photographed for a Ministry of Culture survey of rooms, with an address by UBIK on the television.

The print is from a compact digital camera, flash off, daylight from a balcony door at the left. An older woman on the sofa irons a shirt on a board set across her knees. A boy of about ten does homework at a folding table. A man stands at the balcony door with his back to the room. Nobody faces the television. On its screen is a portrait of UBIK above an empty podium, small and slightly blurred; a caption strip below it is too pixelated to read.

The label on the back, printed: MINISTRY OF CULTURE · ROOMS OF THE REPUBLIC · SURVEY 2012. Typed entries: "Ring Estate, block 2, plot 17. 4 rooms. 3 persons present." In ballpoint: "TV on throughout. Family asked to carry on." The file number was cut off when the print was trimmed.

UNT's playout log of the year before records an address sent without a picture (UNT/SCR/2011/0001). This one has a picture. The date of the broadcast is not on the print. The rest of the survey has not been found; this print was in a folder of returned loans at the UNT television centre.
