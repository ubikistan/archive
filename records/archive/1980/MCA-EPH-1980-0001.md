A Ministry of Culture sound store card for the second pressing of Die Signalgruppe, opened February 1980.

A buff record card from the Ministry of Culture's sound store in the basement of the UNT television centre. Typed: "ITEM: Gramophone records, 30 cm. DIE SIGNALGRUPPE, second pressing. RECEIVED: 02.1980. QTY: whole run, see delivery note. CARTONS: sealed." The delivery note is not attached and is not in the Ministry's files.

The ISSUES section has one line, in ink: "1 copy. To: Directorate for Scientific Development, staff library, Site 6. Authority:" followed by a signature that cannot be read. Below it is a violet stamp, NO FURTHER ISSUE, and a second, fainter impression of the same stamp a little lower.

This contradicts Figures of the Republic, which says the Ministry bought the entire second pressing and never distributed it. The card records one copy leaving the store. The band had played the Station 6 staff evening the year before (IAC/PH/1979/0001). The Station 6 records went to the State Archive under seal in 1987 (IAC/DOC/1987/0001); no gramophone record is listed among them.

In pencil at the bottom: "Stock checked 06.1987. Correct."
