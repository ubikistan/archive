The typed weather script for the late bulletin on UNT channel 1, 13 November 1980, with an amendment asked for and not made.

One sheet, typed. Heading: UNT NEWS 1 · WEATHER · 22.00 · 13.11.80. Text: "Tonight on the plain, dry and clear. Wind from the west, light. Frost on the Weather Ridge after midnight, clearing by nine. The Glasshouses are advised to close their vents." The reader's initials are typed in the corner as "M.T."

Across the second and third lines, in pencil: "Directorate advises no frost. Amend." Below it, in blue ink and another hand: "Ridge reading stands. Read as typed." The ink is initialled; the initials are not legible. The violet stamp at the foot reads UNT CONTINUITY · AS READ and is dated 13.11.80.

The Directorate is not named further. The State Directorate for Scientific Development, which ran Station 6, is the only directorate of that period in the Archive's files; nothing on the sheet says it was that one, and no advice from it is attached. The script for 14 November is not in the binder, and UNT's files do not record whether there was frost on the Ridge.

The binder holds the weather scripts for November 1980 from the UNT television centre. This is the only sheet in it with a note on it.
