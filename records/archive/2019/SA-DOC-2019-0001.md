CENSOR-0 opens a file on Darkstone Enterprises one week after its chairman's shareholder address in Zurich on 14 October 2019, the last occasion on which Aurelian Voss is confirmed to have appeared in person.

The note records three observations from the footage of subsequent addresses: the room does not change, nobody stands beside him, and the lamp is lower than in the previous year. A fourth paragraph states a working hypothesis and is classified unconfirmed. It is not reproduced here.

The note is the first document in DS-0001 and gives the file its reason: "a forecaster large enough to move what it forecasts". The file has not been closed.
