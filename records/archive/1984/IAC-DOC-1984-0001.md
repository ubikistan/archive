Logical arrangement of the Cognitive Array: three rings of stations, for acquisition, synthesis and output, around a centre.

Revision C (1981) labels the centre SUBJECT U-00. Revision D (1984) strikes the label through and pastes FORECAST CORE / U-00 over it. The revision table records the change and gives no reason.
