The notes read NO ISSUER, SETTLEMENT WITHOUT TREASURY, IDENTITY ABSENT, WHO SIGNS? and ASK AIXBT. The paper itself was published outside the Republic on 31 October 2008 (see [the world outside](#/lore/the-world-outside)).
