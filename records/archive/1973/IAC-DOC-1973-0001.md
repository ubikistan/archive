Five subjects of Project INTERFACE connected to one correlator. Three output traces from separate channels, circled in red.

The subjects are two humans (H-01, H-04), a primate (C-02), a dog (D-07) and a bird (A-12). The traces should not match and do. The pencil note is the first known use of the word for what the Institute later called the Convergence Effect. The hand is not identified.
