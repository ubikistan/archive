A citizen's reply card to the Public Works campaign of 1969, received by the Ministry of Culture and held.

The card was the tear-off strip of the campaign leaflet that went with the poster UBIKISTAN NEEDS WHAT YOU CAN BUILD (MCA/POS/1969/0031). Printed heading: MINISTRY OF CULTURE · PUBLIC WORKS · WHAT CAN YOU BUILD? Three ruled lines: NAME, ADDRESS, I CAN BUILD.

Filled in, in ink: "R. Holub". "The Glasshouses, hut 3." Under I CAN BUILD: "A roof that does not leak. Have built two." The violet stamp reads RECEIVED · MINISTRY OF CULTURE, dated 11.1969, and a numbering stamp beside it gives 0211.

In pencil across the top: "Pass to Public Works." Under it, in another hand: "No such office. Hold." The card was not answered. Nothing on it says whether the roof was built.

It was in a carton in the Ministry of Culture's store at the UNT television centre with about forty others, all numbered, all marked Hold. The lowest number in the carton is 0187. Cards 0001 to 0186 are not in the Archive.
