An internal Darkstone memorandum listing four "digital national experiments" in which the firm registers an interest, obtained by SIGNAL-04 in late 2024 and filed by CENSOR-0 in DS-0001.

Three of the four entries are struck through in the obtained copy. The fourth is the Republic. The reason given beside it is a single line: *forecast ledger not held*.

The three struck-through experiments no longer exist. The file does not say what Darkstone's interest in them consisted of, or whether striking through means the interest ended or was satisfied.
