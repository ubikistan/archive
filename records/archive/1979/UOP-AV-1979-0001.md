Film record of the first standard-raising on the lunar surface, attributed to the orbital programme. The pose of the figure at the close of the reel was adopted without alteration for the central portrait of the 50 UBIK note (Series A). The accompanying pencil study traces the silhouette used by the engraver. Provenance of the reel is folk; no official copy is held. Entered as apocrypha pending verification.
