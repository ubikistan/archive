Second sheet of an NICC list of surplus equipment sold off on 14 May 1988, including sixty reels of tape from Site 6.

Carbon copy, columns LOT · DESCRIPTION · QTY · CONDITION · BUYER. Lot 7: "Tape drive cabinets", 2, "working". Lot 8: "Line printer", 1, "faulty". Lot 9: "Magnetic tape, 1/2 in., 10.5 in. reels, ex Site 6", 60, "ERASED". Lot 10: "Chairs, typist", 14. In the buyer column of lot 9 four NICC payroll numbers are written in ink, all with the prefix NS, used for the night shift. The word "sold" is written beside them.

A pencil line under lot 9 reads "reels 41 to 60 not done, see F". It has been struck through in the same pencil. Nobody has initialled the strike.

The Institute's records went to the State Archive under seal in 1987 (IAC/DOC/1987/0001). This list puts sixty reels of its tape on a sale table at NICC a year later. Sheets 1 and 3 are not in the file. The four payroll numbers are not matched to names anywhere the Archive can read.
