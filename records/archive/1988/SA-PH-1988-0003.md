The four founding partners of the risk desk that became Darkstone, New York, 1988. Three faces have been cut out of the print with a blade. The fourth is Voss, 39, who already looked older than he was. The State Archive's copy arrived cut; who cut it is not recorded. The three partners left before 1998 with agreements not to speak.
