Register of Public Enterprises, certificate No. 0001. UBIK Systems Corporation is entered as a public enterprise of the Republic and takes over NICC.

Kept by the Corporation, transferred to the State Archive in 2008 with the company papers. The registrar's signature is illegible and the register does not record the name. The stamp overlaps the folio number, so the folio is read as 1 from the register itself.
