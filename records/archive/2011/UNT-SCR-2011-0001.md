The as-run playout log for UNT channel 1, evening of 7 June 2011, printed from the continuity desk.

A printout of the channel 1 playout screen in five columns: TIME · ITEM · SOURCE · DUR · STATUS. The evening falls in the enrolment weeks for UBIK ID.

20:58 "Ident", AS RUN. 21:00 "Address. UBIK." Source: EXT / UBK LINK 2. Status: NO SIGNAL. 21:00 "Test card (fallback)", AS RUN, 04:12. 21:04 "Address. UBIK. AUDIO ONLY", source EXT / UBK LINK 2, AS RUN, 06:40. 21:11 "PIF: How to identify an unregistered agent", AS RUN. Then the news.

The 21:04 line is highlighted in yellow. In ballpoint beside it: "picture?" Under it, in another hand: "none sent". The schedule planned for that night, which would show whether a picture was expected, is not with the printout.

Footer: printed 08.06.2011, continuity desk 2. The address itself was not kept by UNT.
