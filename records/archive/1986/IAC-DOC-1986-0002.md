Page 2 of the report on run F-6/86-112. At 02:51 the displays in rings 2 and 3 updated together without operator input and the printer ran at four times its rate.

The run was stopped at 03:12. What happened to U-00 is redacted; the report records no injury. The output was sealed by order of the Director of Special Systems. Pages 3 to 6 are withdrawn and page 1 and page 7 have not been found.
