The Ministry of State Affairs' file copy of its 1997 reply to Pavel Oric on public institutions written as programs.

The Ministry of State Affairs' file copy of its reply to Pavel Oric, September 1997.

Typed: "Ref. your submission of 06.1997, 'An office without officials'. The Ministry has read the submission. It considers the proposal premature. It will not be taken further this year. The Ministry thanks you for writing." The word "premature" is typed over another word struck through with x's; three letters of the struck word can be read, "imp". The signature block reads "for the Minister of State Affairs". The signature does not come through on the carbon.

In pencil at the foot, in a different hand: "File as impractical. Copy ASG." The routing slip pinned to the top is ticked for the Advanced Systems Group and dated 10.1997. Nothing on it says whether the copy was read.

Figures of the Republic says the Ministry called the idea impractical in 1997. On the copy that was sent, if the carbon is faithful, the word is premature, and impractical appears only in the file note. Oric's submission is not with the carbon. The Ministry's second reply, of 2015, is not in the Archive either.
