A marshal's checkpoint sheet from kilometre 40 of the Tour of the Plain, June 2007.

Heading, printed: SPORTING COMMITTEE · TOUR OF THE PLAIN · CHECKPOINT 40 · FIELD TRIAL. Four columns: RIDER · FIELD · MARSHAL · INITIAL. The FIELD column was printed before the sheet was handed out and the marshal filled in the rest by hand. For most of the 38 riders the two times agree to within four seconds. Rider 9 has a marshal's time and nothing in the FIELD column. Rider 31 has a FIELD time of 11:02:17; in the marshal's column is written "not seen", and beside it "dust?".

The foot of the sheet has the note "results from MARSHAL column only. Do not publish FIELD." and the stamp SPORTING COMMITTEE · CHECKED. The initials beside the stamp are not legible. The published results of 2007 are not in the Archive, so it cannot be said whether rider 31 finished.

The sheet was in a Motor Yard storeroom in a box of marshals' armbands, with a second checkpoint sheet, kilometre 12, which has no FIELD column.
