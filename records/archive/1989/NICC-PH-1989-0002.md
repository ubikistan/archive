Four NICC night-shift technicians play in a works canteen with the tables pushed back, February 1989.

The print is a machine print from a colour negative, with the laboratory's date FEB 89 on the back. Flash, from the front, so the room behind falls away to dark. On the left a man with a beard plays a bass guitar. Beside him a woman in a white NICC coat over a jumper stands at a trolley holding two reel-to-reel decks. A younger man sits at a drum machine on a canteen table. The fourth, at the right edge, has his back half turned and holds a microphone. Perhaps a dozen people sit at the tables in front, out of focus.

On the back, in pencil: "PI. Canteen B. NS social." Under it, four first names: "Teo, Dana, Vik, ?". In ink, in another hand: "reels from lot 9". Lot 9 of the NICC disposal sale of May 1988 was sixty reels of tape ex Site 6, bought by four night-shift payroll numbers (NICC/DOC/1988/0003).

The leader label on the reel nearest the camera can be read in part. It carries the number 41. The rest of the label is in the flash glare.
