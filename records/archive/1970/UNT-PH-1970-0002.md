UNT's weather observing hut on the Weather Ridge, October 1970, with an observer at the instrument screen.

A small square print. The hut is timber on a concrete plinth, one window, a stovepipe. To its left stands a slatted instrument screen on four legs, and behind it a lattice mast with a cup anemometer at the top. A man in a long coat and a knitted hat stands at the open screen with a notebook, his back three-quarters to the camera. Below the ridge the plateau falls away into haze. The sky is overexposed and has gone white.

On the back, in pencil: "Ridge, for weather. 10.70". Under it, in a second hand: "M.T. at the screen". The stamp reads UNT PICTURE LIBRARY · RETURN AFTER USE.

The initials M.T. are typed as the reader's on the weather script of 13 November 1980 (UNT/DOC/1980/0001). UNT's files do not say whether the observer and the reader were the same person. No staff list for the weather desk of 1970 survives. The print was in a library envelope with no other contents, filed under W at the UNT television centre.
