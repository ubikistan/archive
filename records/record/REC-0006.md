A community edition of UBIK's daily forecasts, published at ubikistan.org/world-state/ and on X as @ubikworldstate, retired once ubik.gold carried the same things itself.

The World State began on 13 October 2026 as a single page built on a snapshot of the engine's preview data. From 21 October it was live at ubikistan.org: the day's synthesis as an edition, every open forecast with its history and resolution rule, the market price beside it where a contract existed, a ledger of settled forecasts with their Brier scores, and a holders' Forecasts page opened with a ubik.gold key. It was not available in the United States or to anyone under 18. @ubikworldstate posted 12 editions, 47 forecasts as they were issued, 14 settlements and two Sunday scorecards.

By November ubik.gold showed each forecast's history, reasoning, external probabilities from Kalshi and Polymarket, and the assets an outcome might touch. On 9 November 2026 the State Archive retired the World State. Its pages now send readers to the same forecast on ubik.gold, and the account stopped posting.

At retirement ubik.gold listed 22 settled forecasts with a mean Brier score of 0.1393. The screenshots were taken on the last day and show the edition built into the page, that of 28 October.
