Part 1

Drop, rings, confluence

A drop is one original post. Each ring is a wider circle of people and a later window of time. A post has about 48 hours of life in For You, so all three rings happen inside that window.

Dropminute 0

One person posts something original that maps to one node of the thesis. It goes on the drop board with an angle, never with text to copy.

Ring 10 to 1 hour

Three core accounts on rota read it and reply with substance: a fact, a source, a question the author can answer. The author answers back. That back-and-forth is the strongest signal the ranker sees.

Ring 21 to 12 hours

The community squad on duty takes it to their own followers with quote posts carrying their own angle, plus derivatives: a meme of the idea, a translation, a chart, a newcomer explainer.

Ring 312 to 48 hours

Outreach. Members bring the idea into conversations already happening elsewhere (AI agents, forecasting, prediction markets) where it genuinely answers something. The idea travels, the drop link sits in a reply.

Confluenceweekly

Each week has one thesis node. Drops on that node, in different formats from different people, overlap. On Sunday the Anchor gathers the best of them into one recap thread or article that quotes the original posts by handle. Old ripples get a second life and newcomers get the whole argument in one place.

Part 2

What the feed rewards in November 2026

X now ranks For You with a Grok-based model that reads each post and predicts how each viewer will react. Its code and weights are public, so most of this is known rather than guessed.

MechanicWhat we do with it
The ranker reads content and predicts replies, reposts, dwell, mutes and blocks per viewer.Posts have to be about something specific. Hype with no content gives the model nothing to match to an interested reader.
Replies outweigh likes. Replies the author engages with are scored separately and count extra.Every drop author stays on the post for the first hour and answers good replies. A drop without an author present is half a drop.
Grok grades reply quality on a 0 to 3 scale. Emoji and one-word replies score near zero. Small accounts get extra spam screening. Replies can be downvoted since March.One real sentence minimum. No "gm", "this", "🚀", ticker-only or AI-written replies. Ten good replies beat a hundred empty ones.
Since 13 July, posts from mutuals surface more in feeds and reply threads.Core and community follow each other. A real community is a mutual graph, and the feed now favours it.
Posts drop out of the For You pool after about 48 hours.All three rings fit inside two days. After that, a post only lives on through quotes and the Sunday confluence.
Replies and reposts don't push a post into strangers' For You feeds.Reach to new people comes from quote posts and original posts. Reposts mostly serve our own followers. Quote with an angle.
Links: sources disagree. Some report a penalty since March, a code read from October finds none.Put links in the first reply until it settles. Costs nothing either way.
Premium replies get a slight priority in conversations.Core accounts run Premium. Community members decide for themselves.
A Community Note is a ranking risk signal.Every claim checkable, every number sourced. One noted post damages trust in the whole thesis.
Coordinated inauthentic activity is against X's rules and X suspends accounts at very large scale.Our protection is that it is real: own words, own timing, varied people per drop, no scripts, no automation.

Part 3

Thesis map

Every drop maps to one node. Five nodes, one per week, so the full thesis cycles every five weeks and each week's ripples converge on the same idea.

NODE 1

The standing agent

UBIK holds a position before anyone asks. It keeps working when nobody is looking. Request-shaped agents wait for a prompt; this one is an entity with a view.

HookWhat the engine noticed today that nobody asked it about.

NODE 2

The record

Forecasts are issued, resolve and get scored against market prices in public. The ledger makes the claim, so nobody has to.

HookSettlement posts. The running score. A miss, shown openly.

NODE 3

Memory that learns

The engine remembers its own forecasts and revises them with reasons. A probability moving from 16% to 22% with the fact that moved it is the product in one line.

HookRevision stories: what changed, and why the number moved.

NODE 4

Media as first application

Characters, clips and a live channel are how the mind meets people. Distribution is part of the product, the lesson AIXBT already taught.

HookA clip, a character moment, the live channel.

NODE 5

The world and the lineage

From AIXBT to UBIK, from Philip K. Dick's novel to Ubikistan. Culture before coin: a community that builds a world around the mind.

HookArchive records, community art, the history.

What does it ingest, what does it remember, does it learn, does it decide on its own, what does the token give access to. Each answered question is a drop. When the honest answer is "not shown yet", say that and name the signal to watch.

Community squads

Split the 50 to 100 accounts into three squads, A, B and C. One squad is on duty each day for ring 2 and rotates. Off-duty members join whenever they actually have something to say. Rotation means each drop draws a different mix of accounts, which reads like what it is: different people who care about the same thing.

Formats

Seven formats, each with its own origin and ripple. Six are dropped to the board. Price talk is never dropped.

Dropped

Update

Origin
Wire, or any member with real news: engine bulletin, shipped feature, settled forecast.
Ring 1
Replies that say what the update changes, tied to a node.
Ring 2
Quote posts explaining it to people who have never heard of UBIK. Translations.
Rule
Facts only. Source in the first reply.
Dropped

Long form and articles

Origin
Anchor weekly, anyone with a finished argument.
Ring 1
Quotes that pull out the one point that landed, in the quoter's own words. Questions for the author.
Ring 2
Short posts that expand a single section for a specific audience (builders, forecasters, traders).
Rule
Articles are the reservoir. Each one cites community posts by handle, so it collects the ripples.
Dropped

Threads

Origin
Threader on Mondays, any member any day.
Ring 1
Replies to specific posts inside the thread with evidence or a counter-question, not only to the first post.
Ring 2
Quote one post from the middle of the thread for a different crowd.
Rule
The first post holds the whole idea on its own. Most readers never open the rest.
Never dropped

Price talk

Origin
Desk and anyone else, as their own read.
Ripple
Organic only. Nobody is asked to respond to price posts.
Content
Context: volume, holder count, how price moved around an engine event. Charts with sources.
Rule
No targets, no "buy", no synchronized price posts. See Rules.
Dropped

Memes

Origin
Studio, anyone with a good one.
Ring 1
Remix chains: quote with your own variation.
Ring 2
Into outside conversations, only where it fits the conversation.
Rule
A meme points at a node. If only insiders get it, it stays in the group chat.
Dropped

Creative projects and concepts

Origin
Community makers: films, art, tools, dashboards, lore.
Ring 1
Core quotes with credit and a specific note on what works.
Ring 2
Featured in the Sunday confluence and filed in the archive.
Rule
Always credit the maker by handle. This is how new makers get pulled in.
Dropped

Team comms

Origin

Ring 1
Wire plus two core accounts answer the obvious newcomer questions under the post, in plain language.
Ring 2
Quote posts that connect the announcement to a node of the thesis.

Never put words in the team's mouth. Nothing beyond what they actually said.
Dropped

Origin
Anyone, when a recurring question gets a real answer.
Ring 1
Replies that add sources or the next question.

Part 6

Drop protocol

A Telegram group with topics: Drops, Outreach (Scout's list), Studio (open assets), Review. The Drops topic only carries drop cards.

DROP · [format] · node [1–5]
Link: [post url]
What it says: [one line]
Angle: [what's worth adding, as a prompt, not a sentence to copy]
Source: [where the facts come from]
Ring 1: [three core names]  ·  Squad on duty: [A/B/C]
  1. Post first, then file the card. The board points at things that exist.
  2. Three drops a day at most. Attention spread over ten posts reaches nobody.
  3. Ring 1 crew reads the post and replies when they have something real to add. Nobody races and nobody posts on a timer.
  4. The author answers replies for the first hour.
  5. The duty squad quotes and makes derivatives through the day, each from their own angle.
  6. Scout posts outside conversations where the idea fits. Members go there as themselves.
  7. Strong ripples get flagged in Review for the Sunday confluence.
Core ruleThe board shares angles, facts and sources. It never shares wording. If two members' replies read alike, the drop card was too specific.

Part 8

Rules

These protect the accounts and the thesis. A throttled account or a Community Note costs more than any single post earns.

Write every post and reply yourself, in your own voice.
Copy text from the board, use reply templates, or have an AI write replies.
Engage when you've read the post and have something to add.
Pile onto a post in the first minutes because it was dropped.
Rotate who responds. Different people on different drops.
The same eight accounts replying to everything in the same order.
Go into outside conversations where the idea answers something.
Drop the ticker under every large account's post.
One account per person.
Alts, bought engagement, automation, scheduled replies, follow-for-follow trains.
Source every number. Show misses as openly as hits.
Claim the engine beats everyone. Let the record say it.
Price talkPrice posts are individual reads and are never dropped to the board or answered on cue. No targets, no "buy now", no posting price takes together. EU rules (MiCA) treat coordinated messaging that sends false or misleading price signals as market manipulation, and that applies to people, not only to bots.
RewardsIf members ever receive anything for posting (tokens, bounties, allowlist spots), those posts need X's paid partnership label or a clear #ad. Holding the token and posting about it unpaid needs no label.
