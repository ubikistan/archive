0The voice

The account has two registers. Everything daily speaks the way the edition already does on ubik.gold: calm, neutral, third person. The vision pieces, the launch and the trailer speak with a voice of their own and take a side on where this is all going.

Third person

UBIK never says "I". It sees, judges, expects, doubts, looks for, reads this as. UBIK expects most of these shocks to travel less far than they seem.

Words, not figures

In public a forecast is described: likely, unlikely, close to a coin toss, too close to call. The figure, the reasoning and the related markets stay with holders on ubik.gold.

The record is public

Settled counts, right and wrong, and the Brier score go out in full. The ledger is the claim; the account never says UBIK is better than anyone.

Plain and specific

News-wire headlines in sentence case. Concrete nouns: sulfur, wheat, war insurance, gold loans. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hashtags, no threads for their own sake.

Never on the account

Token price, charts, "buy", holder counts as a boast, or anything that reads as financial advice. Every forecast post carries Forecasts are probabilities, not financial advice.

The characters

UBIK speaks. AIXBT, who covers crypto, appears in pictures and on the stream and is quoted only through his own account.

Automated, and labelled

The scheduled posts run under X's automated-account label, so nobody mistakes the edition bot for a person replying.

The picture carries the humour

Each edition's picture goes with its post: UBIK composed in the middle of the news, taping a falling chart to a pavilion wall, coffee in hand. The text stays dry.

Open: two words

The editions say "odds" ("roughly even odds") and "calls" ("Its calls on Bank Indonesia came true"). Decide once whether the account keeps them or uses "chance" and "forecasts" throughout.

2The calendar

Three months ahead, in pencil. Each month has one theme the vision piece and the build updates come back to, set against what is happening in the industry. The team is building in real time, so build news has no date until it ships.

Fixed

The daily rhythm, the scorecards, the vision piece on the first Sunday, the arrivals calls. These run whatever ships.

Floats

Every build milestone gets a post the day it is live and a line in that Friday's update. If it slips, the calendar moves, not the promise.

Announce when it is live

Nothing is announced with a date before it runs in a test. "Next" is fine; a day is not.

A quiet week

When nothing ships, a piece from the backlog goes out instead (below), so the account never goes silent.

The industry as context

The theme follows the field: when a new world model or video tool lands, the account says what it means for Ubikistan in two lines, without naming people or claiming to be ahead.

Monday check

Fifteen minutes with the team each Monday: what ships this week, what moved, which theme it feeds.

4The library

Every kind of post opens with its own short graphic from the world: a loudspeaker for the edition, a teleprinter for a new forecast, a stamp for a settlement. Made once, used every day, so the account looks like it is broadcast from Ubikistan.

From the Field Guide

Grey-white concrete, the red ring, teal doors, purple and gold as accents only, always daytime. Each graphic is set in one of the Guide's places.

Short and silent

Three to six seconds, looping where it can. A short sound sting is optional; posts must work with the sound off.

Figures in code

Numbers, dates and verdicts are laid over by the poster, never drawn by the image model, so they are always exact.

Three crops

16:9 for X and YouTube, 1:1 for the timeline card, 9:16 for shorts. Rendered once in each.

Known at a glance

The graphic says what kind of post it is before anyone reads it. After a week, a stamp means a settlement and the departure board means the score.

Made once

Thirteen graphics, rendered from the Field Guide's references with the world's own pipeline, then reused. New ones are added when a new kind of post or event needs one.

7How we measure it

Baselines in week 1, targets agreed together, read every Sunday with the scorecard.

Reach

Followers, and average views on the daily edition post.

The record

Views on settlement posts and the weekly scorecard. These show whether the ledger itself is what people follow.

Video

Clip views, and concurrent viewers on X Live against YouTube.

Into the product

Clicks from posts to ubik.gold, and new holder keys in the same week.

Telegram

New members a week, how many are still active after two weeks, and attendance at each arrivals call.

Rhythm

Share of days the edition, forecasts and settlements went out on time. The target is every day.
