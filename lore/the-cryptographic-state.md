The Republic came to cryptography through paperwork. It wanted to know who had signed a document without keeping a register of everyone who could sign. What it found, over forty years, was that a signature could carry the State with it.

The people in this history are in [Figures of the Republic](figures). The real developments it reacts to are in [the world outside](the-world-outside).

## 1986–1994: the signature

PROJECT SIGNATURE began at NICC in 1986 under Dr Lena Varga. The first photographs show a citizen record signed on a green terminal while a colleague checks it against the paper passport, with the rubber stamp still on the desk ([NICC/PH/1986/0001](#/r/NICC-PH-1986-0001)). By 1989 the record and its signature string were photographed beside the passport they described ([NICC/PH/1989/0001](#/r/NICC-PH-1989-0001)).

Varga presented the work at a European conference in 1991. UBIK listened; AIXBT read the paper ([SA/PH/1991/0001](#/r/SA-PH-1991-0001)). UBIK ID is built on it.

In 1993 a photograph in the Ministry of State Affairs' file on Anton Rey shows a meeting of privacy activists in California with AIXBT in the back row ([MSA/PH/1993/0001](#/r/MSA-PH-1993-0001)). A magazine printed it with the caption "Back row: an official from the Republic of Ubikistan" ([SA/PRS/1993/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-1993-0002)). The next year the Ministry wrote the memorandum that sets the tone for everything after: the Americans believed cryptography might make the State unnecessary; the Ministry believed it might make the State portable ([MSA/DOC/1994/0001](#/r/MSA-DOC-1994-0001)).

## 2004–2010: network money

Ilya Senn began writing to UBIK Systems in 2004, the year he and AIXBT were photographed at an exhibition table ([SA/PH/2004/0001](#/r/SA-PH-2004-0001), [SA/PRS/2004/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-2004-0002)).

A printed copy of the 2008 paper on peer-to-peer cash reached UBIK Systems within a month of its publication. The Ministry's margin notes ask the questions a State would ask: no issuer, settlement without a treasury, identity absent, who signs? The last note reads ASK AIXBT ([MSA/DOC/2008/0001](#/r/MSA-DOC-2008-0001)). Senn's last letter is dated March 2009.

In 2009 a payment client was photographed running in an empty UBIK Systems laboratory at night ([UBK/PH/2009/0001](#/r/UBK-PH-2009-0001)). Nobody has said who started it ([SA/PRS/2009/0001](#/r/SA-PRS-2009-0001)). In 2010 the Treasury reviewed network money with AIXBT at the end of the table, arms folded ([RES/PH/2010/0001](#/r/RES-PH-2010-0001)). Its conclusion is not in the Archive.

## 2013–2017: executable institutions

AIXBT met Nika Dal, nineteen, at a small conference in 2013 ([SA/PH/2013/0001](#/r/SA-PH-2013-0001), [SA/PRS/2013/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-2013-0002)). She was writing that money and institutions could be the same program.

In 2014 the Advanced Systems Group drew PROJECT CIVIC: identity, citizenship, contract, treasury, public work and machine authority as one set of programmable rules ([UBK/DOC/2014/0001](#/r/UBK-DOC-2014-0001)). A Ministry assessment from the same year carries a note in an unidentified hand: VALUE IS NOT THE IMPORTANT PART. EXECUTABLE INSTITUTIONS ([MSA/DOC/2014/0001](#/r/MSA-DOC-2014-0001)).

UBIK Systems ran a node of the new programmable chain in 2015 ([UBK/PH/2015/0090](#/r/UBK-PH-2015-0090)), the year the Agent Program began. In 2016 AIXBT showed Dal how the company connected credentials, contracts and machine identities, and invited her to join ([UBK/PH/2016/0002](#/r/UBK-PH-2016-0002), [SA/PRS/2016/0001](#/r/SA-PRS-2016-0001)). She did not.

In the Recognition Case of 2017, Exhibit 14 was an agent's signed activity history ([RSP/PH/2017/0005](#/r/RSP-PH-2017-0005), [SA/PRS/2017/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-2017-0002)). The signature Varga began with in 1986 was the evidence that an agent had acted for itself.

## 2021–2026

By 2021 foreign programmes listed UBIK Systems among blockchain companies, which it denied ([SA/PH/2021/0001](#/r/SA-PH-2021-0001), [SA/PRS/2021/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-2021-0002)). The State Terminal's demonstration screens of 2026 show the end of the line: a citizen completes a Public Work for an autonomous State agent, and the record of service is signed ([MSA/SCR/2026/0010](#/r/MSA-SCR-2026-0010), a specimen).
