---

- **National football team.** FIFA: unrecognised. One recorded squad photograph, 1984.
- **Tour of the Plain.** Gravel race across the volcanic plain since 1973. AIXBT presents the trophy. The next edition carries the stamp TOUR OF THE PLAIN 2027.
- **Machine Chess, 1988.** Later told as the first human-machine sporting event.
- **The Ubikistan Games.** Track and field. No swimming: there is no coast, and the only pool belongs to the space programme.
