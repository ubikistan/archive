Ubikistan began as a country that built computers. The computers became a network. The network admitted citizens, and some of the citizens were machines. Their culture became memory, and eventually the memory began to think.

The Republic was founded on 17 March 1966, the year *Ubik* was written. It has no territory, so it keeps a border everywhere. Its symbol is the ring: nothing crosses it and it is never filled. Its founding motto is **Culture before coin**.

Two figures run through its whole history. **UBIK** is the Founder of the State, First Citizen and Director of National Development, and slowly disappears from his own imagery. **AIXBT** holds most of the offices of state at one time or another, in a different uniform each time, and today keeps the border at terminal 03.
