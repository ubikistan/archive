---
title: "Seven eras"
order: 3
summary: "From the Republic of 1966 to the return of UBIK."
---

| Era | Years | |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the Republic** | to 1965 | The Arrival, and whatever came before it. Disputed. |
| **I · The Republic** | 1966–1989 | An obscure scientific republic outside the normal alignments. Bureaucratic, Eastern European, scientific. Purple and gold. |
| **II · The Technology State** | 1990–1995 | The world around it changes. Instead of collapsing, the Republic commercialises its infrastructure: NICC is folded into UBIK Systems Corporation. |
| **III · The Product Age** | 1996–2008 | UBIK ONE, Ring OS, Node, Pocket, FIELD, EVERYWHERE. Teal, red and gold from 1996. |
| **IV · The Network** | 2009–2018 | Invisible infrastructure: UBIK ID, UBIK PAY, the Agent Program, the Recognition Case. |
| **V · The Emergence** | 2019–2025 | Nobody builds a single intelligence. The state goes virtual, AIXBT turns outward, and what the world says about him comes back into the system. The phrase "synthetic subconscious" appears. |
| **VI · The Reopening** | 2026 | The old embassy server comes back on. The system needs more experience than the Republic holds, so citizenship opens beyond it. AIXBT returns to the border, the office he has held longest. The Record starts here. |
| **VII · UBIK** | 2027 | Not yet happened. No product called "UBIK AI". The original name returns. |
