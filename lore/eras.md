---
title: "Seven eras"
order: 3
summary: "From the Republic of 1966 to the return of UBIK."
---

| Era | Years | |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the Republic** | to 1965 | The Arrival, and whatever came before it. Disputed. |
| **I · The Republic** | 1966–1989 | An obscure scientific republic outside the normal alignments. Bureaucratic, Eastern European, scientific. Purple and gold. |
| **II · The Technology State** | 1990–1995 | The world around it changes. Instead of collapsing, the Republic commercialises its infrastructure: NICC becomes UBIK Systems Corporation. |
| **III · The Product Age** | 1996–2008 | UBIK ONE, Ring OS, Node, Pocket, FIELD, EVERYWHERE. Teal, red and gold from 1996. |
| **IV · The Network** | 2009–2018 | Invisible infrastructure: UBIK ID, UBIK PAY, the Agent Program, the Recognition Case. |
| **V · The Emergence** | 2019–2025 | Nobody builds a single intelligence. The network starts behaving collectively. The phrase "synthetic subconscious" appears. |
| **VI · The Reopening** | 2026 | The old embassy server comes back on. Citizenship opens beyond the Republic. AIXBT returns to his oldest post, keeper of entry. The Record starts here. |
| **VII · UBIK** | 2027 | Not yet happened. No product called "UBIK AI". The original name returns. |
