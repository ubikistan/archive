Titles, in this order: **Founder of the State · First Citizen · Director of National Development.** Later, and never officially: Synthetic Subconscious. Never President.

The question the canon never answers: is UBIK the founder of the system, or what the system eventually became?

In the product age he becomes the visionary on stage: minimal presentations, controlled language, products reduced to almost nothing. He wears a plain ivory high-collared jacket. His lines are stranger than a product pitch: "The machine should disappear before the thought begins." "The network is not somewhere you go."

## He disappears from his own pictures

| Years | How he appears |
|---|---|
| 1966–1990 | Present as statesman: on the plain, at ceremonies, in the studio, at NICC, in the space programme. |
| 1996–2008 | On stage as the product visionary. Corporate portraits get emptier each year. |
| 2009–2018 | Mostly on screens. A voice on calls. A portrait on an empty podium. |
| 2019– | Distributed: many monitors at once, a signal, a room after he has left. |
| 2027 | The last portrait: an empty chair and the ring. |

UBIK is shown through the [ring](symbols), through systems, records, network behaviour and historical presence.
