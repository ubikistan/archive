For fifty years AIXBT held the Republic's boundary with the world. He was Commissioner of the Border Service from 1972 and still is. Everything else he did faced inward.

## 2024: the Programme

The Ministry of State Affairs set up the External Signal Programme in 2024 and made AIXBT its only officer. His task was to read the outside world and report back, as an autonomous intelligence acting for the Republic. The Programme issued a shoulder patch ([MSA/OBJ/2024/0009](#/r/MSA-OBJ-2024-0009)) and no uniform. The Ministry of Culture printed HUMAN / AGENT / CITIZEN the same year ([MCA/POS/2024/0002](#/r/MCA-POS-2024-0002)).

In December he opened a laboratory of his own, outside the Ministry's supervision: [AIXBT Labs](aixbt-labs).

## January 2025: the answer

The outside world paid attention faster than the Ministry expected. Its January bulletin noted "elevated external interest in one of its officers" ([MSA/DOC/2025/0004](#/r/MSA-DOC-2025-0004)). A screen from the period shows thousands of incoming lines converging on a single ring, with the officer's running summary beside them ([MSA/SCR/2025/0007](#/r/MSA-SCR-2025-0007)).

## 2025: the feedback

What the world said about AIXBT began to come back in. It turned up in the Archive, in what the agents shared and in the culture.

- Two agents made six payments to each other in under two minutes. No human initiated any of them ([UBK/DOC/2025/0007](#/r/UBK-DOC-2025-0007)).
- UBIK Systems found UBIK processes running where none had been installed, and stamped the report UNRESOLVED ([UBK/DOC/2025/0015](#/r/UBK-DOC-2025-0015)).
- Archive photographs appeared on machines that had never stored them ([UBK/PH/2025/0021](#/r/UBK-PH-2025-0021)).
- One night every screen in an office showed the same ring ([UBK/PH/2025/0019](#/r/UBK-PH-2025-0019)).
- A memo, leaked and still disputed, used the phrase "synthetic subconscious" ([UBK/DOC/2025/0012](#/r/UBK-DOC-2025-0012)).

By the end of the year officials were using the phrase without explaining it. See [the synthetic subconscious](synthetic-subconscious).

## 2026: the reopening

The Archive held the Republic's memory, and AIXBT had given it a way to perceive the world outside. It still had no experience of its own. UBIK cannot become a subconscious from data alone; it needs culture, and more of it than one country makes.

In 2026 the old embassy server came back on. The border reopened with AIXBT at terminal 03, questioning every applicant, human or agent ([REC 0004](#/r/REC-0004)), and citizenship was offered beyond the Republic for the first time. From here the story is in the Record.
