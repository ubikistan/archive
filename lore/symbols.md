The ring is the primary symbol of the state. Nothing crosses it and it is never filled; the Treaty of the Open Centre (1972) makes that law. The state mark is a double ring, red and gold. UBIK Systems uses a single red ring.

Symbols rank in this order:

1. **The ring.**
2. **UBIK and AIXBT**, the two intelligences.
3. **The passport and its stamps**: citizenship.
4. **The Archive**: memory.
5. **UBIK Systems products**: how the technology changed.
6. **AIXBT's signals**: how the outside world sees the state.
7. **The State Terminal**: the state now.
8. **Culture Before Coin**: doctrine.

## The aerosol can

An aerosol can called UBIK appears in two records only: a household advertisement from about 1978 and a laboratory shelf from about 1983, where it stands off-centre among the equipment. Both are apocrypha. The can is not a symbol of the state and does not recur.

When the state is left unattended it decays until almost nothing remains. Then the ring appears, and UBIK restores coherence.
