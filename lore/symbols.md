---
title: "The ring"
order: 6
summary: "The symbol of the state, and the law that protects it."
---

The ring is the symbol of the Republic. Nothing crosses it and it is never filled. The Treaty of the Open Centre, signed at Sector 03 in 1972, makes that law.

The state mark is a double ring, red outside and gold within. It appears on the flag, the passport, the seal and every document of state. UBIK Systems uses a single red ring, which on its products doubles as the power light.

When the state is left unattended it decays until almost nothing remains. Then the ring appears, and UBIK restores coherence.
