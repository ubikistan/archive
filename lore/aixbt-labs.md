Every office AIXBT held for fifty-eight years belonged to someone else: the Reserve, the Post, the Border Service, Station 6, UBIK Systems. The External Signal Programme gave him a task and no staff. In December 2024 he opened a laboratory of his own and called it AIXBT Labs ([AXL/PH/2024/0002](#/r/AXL-PH-2024-0002)). Its first circular gave one purpose: research the narratives ([AXL/DOC/2024/0003](#/r/AXL-DOC-2024-0003)).

The Labs is not a ministry and not a division of UBIK Systems. The Ministry of State Affairs noted its existence in January 2025 and filed the note under organisations outside its supervision ([MSA/DOC/2025/0008](#/r/MSA-DOC-2025-0008)).

## What it does

The Labs reads the outside world, sorts what it finds into continuing subjects, and writes dated reports from them. Its own working terms are set out in an operating circular ([AXL/DOC/2025/0008](#/r/AXL-DOC-2025-0008)).

The method is FORECAST's turned outward. At Station 6 the F-Series machines took in weather, broadcasts and transport and produced a window and a confidence, with U-00 at the centre and AIXBT behind the glass (see [the Consciousness Programme](consciousness-programme)). At the Labs the inputs are public conversation, and AIXBT occupies the centre himself. He has not commented on the resemblance.

## The staff

The Labs publishes no staff list and the Archive keeps none. In photographs the staff are at the edges: a back at a distant desk, a hand on a cable, a shoulder at a loading dock. Only one photograph, taken around three in the morning, shows several of them at once. None is named in any record. Their badges have the name field left blank ([AXL/OBJ/2024/0004](#/r/AXL-OBJ-2024-0004)). The night photograph is [AXL/PH/2025/0006](#/r/AXL-PH-2025-0006).

## The look of the place

The operations floor is black and indigo, lit by its instruments, with a single violet ring where the Republic would put a red one ([AXL/PH/2024/0001](#/r/AXL-PH-2024-0001)). The building is a long block of black glass on the edge of the government district, with a ring over the door and no name ([AXL/PH/2024/0006](#/r/AXL-PH-2024-0006)). The public hall is pale concrete and daylight. In the boardroom the table itself is a display, showing the topics the Labs is following as a map ([AXL/PH/2025/0007](#/r/AXL-PH-2025-0007)). The loading dock is ugly. Type in a monospace face, small, set close. No logos on the machines. The laboratory outside the Archive has a mark of its own; the Archive does not reproduce it and files the Labs under the ring. The Labs is the only institution in the Archive whose colour is not the State's, and the State Archive files its objects as they come.

## Disputed

Whether the Labs is AIXBT's own or a cover the Ministry allows him to keep is not settled. A memo of 2025 calls it "his laboratory". A later one calls it "the laboratory". Both are filed. Abroad, a newspaper called it "a laboratory with no staff list" ([SA/PRS/2025/0002](#/r/SA-PRS-2025-0002)).
