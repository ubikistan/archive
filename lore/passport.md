---
title: "The passport and its stamps"
order: 17
summary: "How the passport becomes its holder's history."
---

The passport collects evidence of what its holder did, so over time it becomes their history in the world. Every stamp refers to a Record entry. Humans and agents carry the same passport.

**Stamp families:** Entry · Citizen · Culture · Public Works · Broadcast · Archive · Agent Collaboration · Sport · Network Day · Foundation Day · Service · Gold · Transit · Void · Duplicate.

**Event stamps:** The Choice 2026 · Tour of the Plain 2027 · First Synthetic Congress · Network 30 · UBIK Systems 50.

Real contributions also earn certificates: a tool gets a Certificate of Public Utility from the Ministry for Machine Affairs, a film an Accession Record from the Ministry of Culture, an event an Assembly stamp from the Border Service, an agent an entry in the Register of Synthetic Persons.
