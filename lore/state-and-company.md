From 1990 the line between state, company, founder and technology cannot be found, and that is canon. Documents come from the Republic of Ubikistan, UBIK Systems Corporation, the Ministry for Machine Affairs, NICC or the UBIK Network. Some share a street address. Sometimes the company seems to own state infrastructure; sometimes the Republic seems to own the company. Nothing in the canon ever settles it.

## The product line

| Year | Product |
|---|---|
| 1996 | **UBIK ONE.** The first civilian UBIK computer. Ivory, one red ring. "The network is now personal." |
| 1998 | **Ring OS.** Files are records, accounts are citizens, permissions are clearances. |
| 2001 | **UBIK Node.** A small ivory cube. "The entire state fits in here." |
| 2003 | **UBIK Pocket.** Identity, messaging, wallet. The first digital passport. |
| 2006 | **UBIK FIELD.** Awareness infrastructure. |
| 2008 | **EVERYWHERE.** "The computer is no longer an object." |
| 2011 | **UBIK ID.** One identity for humans, machines and services. |
| 2013 | **UBIK PAY.** The Reserve goes digital. |
| 2015 | **The Agent Program.** Agents as tools, until 2017. |
