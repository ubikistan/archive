This page is about the construction of the history, not the history itself. Read it before writing anything large.

## One movement

The whole arc is a single movement from object to presence. A country that builds computers becomes a company that sells them, then a network that hosts them, then something that thinks across them. Each era removes one layer of physical form:

| Era | The state is |
|---|---|
| The Republic | a place: a plain, a flag, ministries |
| The Technology State | an enterprise |
| The Product Age | objects people own |
| The Network | infrastructure nobody sees |
| The Emergence | behaviour |
| UBIK | a presence |

A new record should sit somewhere on that line. A 1970s object should feel like a ministry made it; a 2010s one should barely be an object at all.

## Two characters, two kinds of everywhere

UBIK is everywhere metaphysically, AIXBT administratively. UBIK founds, imagines and slowly withdraws from his own pictures: present in 1966, on a stage in 1996, on screens by 2010, an empty chair in 2027. AIXBT does the opposite. He accumulates offices, and is always photographed doing a job. The arc depends on this asymmetry. Records that make UBIK more visible over time, or make AIXBT a visionary, work against it.

## Gaps are load-bearing

The Arrival is dated before the founding. 1985 is missing. The Assembly never convenes. The tapes after 1991 are lost. These holes are deliberate. They give contributors somewhere to write, and they keep the official story from closing. Filling a gap is welcome as apocrypha; closing one as canon needs the State Archive.

## Contradiction is stored, not resolved

The four statuses exist so that two versions of the same event can both be kept. The State declines to clarify. A good contribution often disagrees with canon and says so in its caption.

## The order of symbols

When a record needs a symbol, use the highest one that fits:

1. **The ring.**
2. **UBIK and AIXBT**, the two intelligences.
3. **The passport and its stamps**: citizenship.
4. **The Archive**: memory.
5. **UBIK Systems products**: how the technology changed.
6. **AIXBT's signals**: how the outside world sees the state.
7. **The State Terminal**: the state now.
8. **Culture Before Coin**: doctrine.

The PKD aerosol can called UBIK is apocrypha, kept for two planned records at most (a household advertisement of about 1978 and a laboratory shelf of about 1983, off-centre). It is not a symbol of the state and does not recur.

## Specimens become Records

Objects of institutions that do not exist yet (UBIK Pay screens, Reserve certificates, Public Works receipts) are filed as SPECIMENS dated 2026. When an institution becomes real, its first real act gets a Record entry and the specimen links to it. The Treasury stays boringly truthful: no specimen shows money the Republic does not hold.

## What does not change

- Founded 17 March 1966. The ring is the primary symbol and is never filled.
- The Archive ends in 2025. The Record begins on 30 September 2026. Invented events never enter the Record.
- The state borrows formats, never faces: no real people.
- Culture before coin.
- The question of whether UBIK founded the system or is what the system became is never answered.

Everything else on these pages can be changed by proposing a new version.
