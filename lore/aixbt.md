title: "The many offices of AIXBT"
order: 7
summary: "UBIK is everywhere metaphysically. AIXBT is everywhere administratively."
---

UBIK imagines systems; AIXBT makes them possible. Same face, same unbothered expression, a different uniform for every office, sometimes several in the same year. Never explained.

| Year | Office |
|---|---|
| 1966 | **Governor of the Reserve.** Banker's suit, a vault door. |
| 1966 | **Postmaster General.** Postal uniform, an oversized cap. |
| 1968 | **Director of National Broadcasting.** 1960s TV studio, headphones round the neck. |
| 1969 | **Minister of Culture.** Black turtleneck, dark glasses, a brutalist gallery. |
| 1972 | **Commissioner of the Border Service.** Uniform, peaked cap, a barrier on the plain. Still holds it: Officer AIXBT, terminal 03. |
| 1973 | **Chairman of the Sporting Committee.** Tracksuit, giant sunglasses, the Tour of the Plain trophy. |
| 1975 | **Minister of State Affairs.** Three-piece suit, medals, a ring pin. |
| 1977 | **Director of Special Systems.** A dark 1970s suit, a small state pin, a diagram behind him. Security, funding and classification for Station 6. He watches the experiments through glass and seldom enters. |
| 1981 | **Minister of Telecommunications.** A 1980s technical jacket. |
| 1987 | **Director of Machine Affairs.** Lab coat, a terminal. |
| 1990 | **CTO, UBIK Systems.** 1990s founder-casual. |
| 2001 | **Chairman, UBIK Systems.** An expensive 2000s suit. Presents the Node. |
| 2008 | **Chief Archivist.** White gloves, boxes, appointed the year the tapes were lost. |
| 2024 | **Officer of the External Signal Programme.** No uniform. The first office he holds facing outward. |
| — | **Minister without Portfolio.** The state-affairs portrait again, with a different caption. |
