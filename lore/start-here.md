The Republic of Ubikistan was founded on 17 March 1966 on a flat volcanic plain. It never existed. This archive is its memory anyway: the photographs, documents, stamps, banknotes, films and press clippings a real state would keep, written straight, as if every word were true.

Everything here is free to copy (CC0). Anyone can read it. Anyone with a GitHub or X account can add to it.

## What is in it

**The Archive** is what Ubikistan says happened, from 1965 to 2025. Every object has a code, such as `MSA/PH/1966/0001`: the institution that issued it, what kind of object it is, the year, and a number. Every object also has a status:

- **Authenticated**: canon.
- **Incomplete**: probably true, with parts missing.
- **Disputed**: the Archive does not agree with itself.
- **Apocrypha**: everything new starts here. Some of the best records stay here.
- **Specimen**: a proposed state object dated 2026, not evidence of anything.

**The Record** is what really happens, from 30 September 2026 on: real films, real events, real work. Records are numbered REC 0001 onward and have no status. They happened.

**Culture** is what citizens make now: films, images, merch, music, writing. It is credited to them and does not have to agree with the Archive.

**Collections** sit beside the main history: stamp sheets, patches and pins, banknotes, the Station 6 dossier.

**Lore** is how the Republic works: its history, its two central figures UBIK and AIXBT, its institutions, its money, its calendar. The **Handbook** (this section) is for contributors.

## Finding your way

- **Search** from the Archive page. It searches codes, titles, dates, institutions and text. Filter by era, institution, kind of object and status.
- **Read a record** for its slate (code, date, era, issuer, format, status), its caption and its links to related records and lore.
- **Follow the eras** from the Republic (1966–1989) through the Technology State, the Product Age, the Network and the Emergence to the Reopening in 2026.
- **Start with these pages**: [the history](#/lore/history), [the master timeline](#/lore/timeline), [UBIK](#/lore/ubik), [the many offices of AIXBT](#/lore/aixbt), and the [Characters](characters) list.

## Taking part

Reading needs no account. Everything that changes the archive needs you to sign in with GitHub or X. A pseudonym is fine. What you add is credited to that account.

| You can | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Vote 👍 or 👎 and leave remarks | **Votes and remarks**, under every page | Shown at once |
| Add a note: a finding, a source, a contradiction | **Notes and findings**, under every record and lore page | Reviewed, then shown beside the official text |
| Propose a stronger version of an image | **Versions**, under every record | Reviewed; then votes decide which version is shown |
| Edit a page | the **Edit** tab on any page | Reviewed, then published; the old version stays in History |
| Submit a new record or your own work | **Submit** in the menu | Reviewed; new Archive records enter as apocrypha |
| Join a project | **[Projects](#/projects)** in the menu | Your handle appears on the project; its lead gets in touch |

The State Archive reviews contributions. Trusted citizens, listed by the State Archive, can have their changes go live without waiting. How it all works in detail: [how the archive is kept](editing).

## The rules, short

1. The Archive is invented history up to 2025. The Record is real, from 2026.
2. Everything new in the Archive starts as apocrypha.
3. Contradictions are welcome. Say what yours contradicts.
4. No real people as part of the history: no photographs, meetings, quotes or endorsements of anyone real. The Republic has its own people.
5. The ring is the symbol.
6. Culture before coin: no prices, no predictions, no financial advice.
7. A new record has to earn its place.
8. Write like an archivist: plain, dry, specific.

The full version is in [the rules](rules).

## For AI agents

The whole archive is one file, `lore.json`, and as plain text in `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`, linked at the foot of every page. Agents working in the repository follow its `CLAUDE.md`.

## Your account and the log

When you sign in, the desk keeps a log of your sign-ins and contributions: your account, your handle, the time and what you did. It keeps no IP addresses. The log is kept for one year and only the State Archive can read it.
