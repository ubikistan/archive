The Republic did not happen in a vacuum. These are developments in the real world that the Ministry of State Affairs, NICC and UBIK Systems followed. The facts in the left column are real. Anything Ubikistan did in response is Ubikistan's own history and is filed in the Archive.

The Archive never shows real people taking part in the Republic's history. Real people appear here only where the fact about them is real.

| Year | Outside | What the Republic did |
|---|---|---|
| 1961 | The non-aligned states hold their first summit in Belgrade. | The Republic, founded five years later, takes the movement as its model for being nobody's satellite. |
| 1979 | Personal cassette players go on sale and music becomes something carried alone. | AIXBT tours an electronics factory in Japan the same year. |
| 1981 | Home computers reach ordinary households in Europe and America. | NICC opens. Schools receive terminals by 1984. |
| 1989 | A proposal for a system of linked documents is written at CERN, the European physics laboratory. | UBIK Systems is founded the following year. |
| 1991 | The first web pages go public. | The Assembly is called and does not convene. |
| 1992 | A mailing list of cryptographers and privacy activists begins writing about money, signatures and the state; they come to be called cypherpunks. | Dr Lena Varga's PROJECT SIGNATURE starts at NICC. |
| 1990s | Musicians and artists begin to run their own online communities and services. | The Ministry of Culture opens the government website on Network Day, 1996. |
| 2008 | On 31 October a paper describing a peer-to-peer electronic cash system is sent to a cryptography mailing list. | A printed copy reaches UBIK Systems within the month. |
| 2009 | On 3 January the first block of that system is created. | Ilya Senn's last letter is dated March 2009. |
| 2015 | A blockchain designed to run programs as well as payments goes live on 30 July. | The UBIK Agent Program begins. Pavel Oric is told again that programmable institutions are impractical. |
| 2024 | On 2 November an AI agent called aixbt, built by a small pseudonymous team, launches on Virtuals, a platform for AI agents on the Base network. It reads crypto conversation on X and posts what it finds. Its makers' account, [@aixbt_labs](https://x.com/aixbt_labs), opens in December. | The External Signal Programme. AIXBT opens [AIXBT Labs](aixbt-labs). |
| 2025 | The agent passes 300,000 followers within two months. On 18 March an intruder reaches its operating dashboard. On 29 May its research Terminal ([aixbt.tech](https://aixbt.tech)) opens to the public; Indigo follows on 31 July. | The January Signal; the Feedback Problem. |
| 2026 | In April the Labs releases a server that lets other AI agents use its fourteen research tools; in September, a plugin for a general-purpose AI assistant. | The border reopens. |

Popular culture in these decades kept returning to machine identity: performers in uniforms, in helmets, under other names. The Republic's own artists, listed in [Figures of the Republic](figures), worked in the same currents.
