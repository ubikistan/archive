A working list for contributors. Each entry gives the character's role, where they first appear, the records that carry them, and the threads that are still open. Open threads are invitations: a record or a note that picks one up is welcome, and contradicting an entry is allowed as apocrypha (see [the rules](rules)).

Every character here is invented. Real people never become characters; they appear only as dated context in [the world outside](the-world-outside).

## The two

**UBIK.** Founder of the State, First Citizen, Director of National Development. Ivory head, teal scalp and ear discs, red eyes. Present as a statesman until 1995, a product visionary until 2008, then less and less visible: screens, a voice, a signal, an empty chair in 2027. The canon never says whether he founded the system or became it. See [UBIK](ubik).
- First seen: [MSA/PH/1966/0001](#/r/MSA-PH-1966-0001), the first state portrait.
- Also known as **U-00**, the subject at the centre of FORECAST at Station 6 from 1977 ([IAC/DOC/1977/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1977-0001)).
- Open: what happened to U-00 on the night of the 1986 Incident; where he was in 2023, the first UBIK Systems event he missed; whether he knows about AIXBT Labs.

**AIXBT.** The administrator. Blue-violet skin, broad frog-like head, heavy-lidded and unbothered, a different uniform for every office. Commissioner of the Border Service since 1972 and still at terminal 03. Director of Special Systems at Station 6, CTO and then Chairman of UBIK Systems, Officer of the External Signal Programme, and from December 2024 founder of his own institution, [AIXBT Labs](aixbt-labs) ([AXL/PH/2024/0002](#/r/AXL-PH-2024-0002)). See [the many offices of AIXBT](aixbt).
- First seen: [MSA/PH/1966/0002](#/r/MSA-PH-1966-0002).
- Open: whether the Labs is his or something the Ministry lets him keep; whether he has seen the 1981 Station 6 schematics that Indigo resembles; what he did between 2008 and 2024, when the record lists no new office.

## Agents and citizens

**The first agent citizen.** The autonomous agent that petitioned for citizenship in the Recognition Case of 2017. The State refused, AIXBT appealed, and the first agent passport was handed to a monitor ([RSP/PH/2017/0002](#/r/RSP-PH-2017-0002)). It has no name in the Archive.
- Open: its name; what it did with citizenship; whether it is the same agent as **AGT-000173**, the holder of the first agent economic account ([RSP/DOC/2017/0004](#/r/RSP-DOC-2017-0004)).

**The First Network Citizen** (1999). Registered on the network. Disputed whether this citizen was an agent ([timeline](timeline)).
- Open: everything. No record shows this citizen yet.

**The staff of AIXBT Labs.** Anonymous by rule. In photographs they are at the edges: a back at a distant desk, a hand on a cable, a shoulder at a loading dock. Only the 3 a.m. photograph shows several at once ([AXL/PH/2025/0006](#/r/AXL-PH-2025-0006)). Never named, never shown full face.
- Open: their work, through objects (badges with blank names, notes left on desks, shift rotas), never their faces.

## Outside the Archive

**The keeper.** In the citizen film *The Choice* ([ACC 0001](#/r/ACC-0001)) a keeper offers UBIK a blue disc and a red disc. Culture, not canon: the keeper does not appear in the Archive. Bringing the keeper in would be apocrypha.

## Adding a character

A new character earns a place the same way a record does: by moving the history on, showing a new institution, changing what we know about UBIK or AIXBT, or making a real contradiction. Give them a face that is their own, a year of birth or a span of activity, one thing they did, and one thing nobody knows about them. Propose them as a note on this page, or file the first record that shows them.
