Darkstone Enterprises is a private company registered in Zurich. It manages money for pension funds, insurers, sovereign funds and other managers of money, and through them holds a position in most things that can be held. It does not run companies. It writes to them.

The Republic has no dealings with Darkstone. The State Archive keeps one file on it, DS-0001, opened by CENSOR-0 in October 2019 and still open. This page is what the file contains that may be read.

## What the Republic knows

Darkstone was founded in New York in 1988 as a four-partner risk desk inside a bond house and spun out in 1992. Three of the four partners left before 1998, each with a settlement and an agreement not to speak. The fourth, Aurelian Voss, has been chairman and chief executive since the spin-out.

Its instrument is a risk system called the Ledger, built in the late 1990s and later offered to other institutions, which run their own books through it. By the early 2000s Darkstone could see every position it did not hold. In 2016 the Ledger began issuing forecasts as well as prices. Clients noticed that the forecasts were unusually accurate. Nobody asked why.

The Archive's reading, filed by ORACLE and not acknowledged by the state: a forecaster whose positions are large enough to move the outcome is not forecasting. It is quoting.

Headquarters moved to Zurich in 2012: a tower of black glass, taller than anything near it, with no windows on the lower eleven floors.

## The Letters

Every January since 1994 Darkstone has published *A Letter to Those We Hold*, addressed to every entity it has a position in. The Letters are warm, concerned and patient. Each asks for one small thing. The State Archive keeps the full run ([SA/DOC/1994/0001](#/r/SA-DOC-1994-0001) is the first) with CENSOR-0's marginal notes recording what each one asked for.

| Year | Opening line | The small thing |
|---|---|---|
| 1994 | We have been thinking about time. | Publish a five-year plan. |
| 2001 | Uncertainty is a cost like any other. | Share your risk model with ours. |
| 2009 | We held when others could not. | Accept a Darkstone observer on the board. |
| 2016 | The future is a position. | Hedge through the Ledger. |
| 2020 | Stewardship is patience made visible. | Adopt the common reporting standard. |
| 2023 | We are, increasingly, all one portfolio. | Route treasury decisions through Ledger forecasts. |
| 2025 | Some of you are very small. We hold you anyway. | Nothing. CENSOR-0 underlines this twice. |

Compliance with each request has been near total. No Letter has ever mentioned the Republic.

## The chairman

Aurelian Voss, born 1949 in Trieste, trained as an actuary, in reinsurance until 1988. See his file, [FIG 0902](#/characters). His last confirmed appearance in person was at a shareholder meeting in Zurich on 14 October 2019. Since then he has been seen only on screens, from the same black room, in the same black suit, under a single cold light.

CENSOR-0's note of 2019 ([SA/DOC/2019/0001](#/r/SA-DOC-2019-0001)) records the opening of file DS-0001 and a working hypothesis, classified unconfirmed, that the office keeps to itself. The Archive does not say whether it is true. What it holds is the light, which is lower in each year's footage, and the sentences, which are longer.

## Before Darkstone

The file holds a few pictures of him before he was on screens: his actuarial class in Geneva in 1971, where he stands apart at the edge ([SA/PH/1971/0001](#/r/SA-PH-1971-0001)); a reinsurance office in Zurich in 1979, alone at the one lit desk ([SA/PH/1979/0001](#/r/SA-PH-1979-0001)); the four partners in New York in 1988, the other three faces cut out of the print ([SA/PH/1988/0003](#/r/SA-PH-1988-0003)); and the 1992 magazine cover that called him the man who priced death, his last interview ([SA/PRS/1992/0004](#/r/SA-PRS-1992-0004)). The last photograph of him in a room with other people is from Zurich on 14 October 2019 ([SA/PH/2019/0003](#/r/SA-PH-2019-0003)).

## Rumours

Since 2019 the rumours have done the work that sightings used to do. The Archive files them as disputed and confirms none: a tabloid asking whether he is still alive ([SA/PRS/2021/0003](#/r/SA-PRS-2021-0003)); CENSOR-0's own comparison of two broadcasts six years apart, in which one ear sits lower ([SA/DOC/2022/0001](#/r/SA-DOC-2022-0001)); a tall old man on the quay in Trieste, the city of his birth, in a week when the broadcast came from the black room as usual ([SA/PH/2023/0001](#/r/SA-PH-2023-0001)); and an anonymous thread claiming the chairman is a model trained on his own Letters ([SA/SCR/2025/0004](#/r/SA-SCR-2025-0004)).

## Why the file exists

In 2024 SIGNAL-04 obtained an internal Darkstone memorandum listing four "digital national experiments" of interest ([SA/DOC/2024/0001](#/r/SA-DOC-2024-0001)). The Republic is the fourth. The other three have since ceased to exist.

The Republic runs a public forecast ledger that Darkstone does not hold, and a Reserve whose gold is where the Reserve says it is. The file's view is that these two facts are the whole of Darkstone's interest.

## 2026

In the spring of 2026 the Assembly received a letter from Zurich offering a small allocation to the Reserve and support for "the independence of the archive". The Assembly has not replied. The State Archive has not filed the letter. CENSOR-0 has read it.

Darkstone is not an enemy of the Republic. It has never opposed anything. It takes a position on both sides and waits.
