From 1971 to 1987 the Republic ran a research programme on one question: is consciousness produced by a brain, or can it exist across systems? The researchers did not think they were building artificial intelligence. They studied cognition, perception, memory, group behaviour, biological signals, prediction and the continuity of identity.

The programme is declassified in part. What follows is sorted by how much the State admits.

## What is public

The **Institute for Applied Consciousness** (IAC) opened in 1971 under the State Directorate for Scientific Development. It worked from **Station 6**, a sealed concrete complex on the plain, described to the public as a neurological and computational research facility. It researched human-machine interaction. In 1987 it was dissolved for "programme consolidation" and its staff moved to NICC ([IAC/DOC/1987/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1987-0001)).

## What is declassified

**Project INTERFACE, 1971–1975.** Human and animal subjects wore non-invasive sensor rigs while early computers tried to correlate their signals: EEG, pulse, visual response, sleep, navigation. Subjects were numbered by kind: H for human, C for primate, D for canine, A for avian ([IAC/DOC/1971/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1971-0001)). Subjects connected through the same machine began to produce correlated patterns. The papers call it **the Convergence Effect** and do not call it anything stronger ([IAC/DOC/1973/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1973-0001)).

**Subject U-00, from 1977.** UBIK visited Station 6 in 1971, took part voluntarily from 1974 and became subject U-00 in 1977. His identification sheet leaves several standard fields empty ([IAC/DOC/1977/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1977-0001)). The researchers recorded that U-00 tolerated machine coupling far better than the human subjects. They did not record why.

**Closed loops.** From 1975 the machines no longer only read the signals. They fed processed signals back: patterns, sound, imagery. Brain to machine, machine to transformed signal, signal back to brain. The question was whether a machine could become part of cognition rather than observe it.

**FORECAST, 1978–1986.** The F-Series machines took in weather, communications, transport, broadcasts, economic data, physiological responses, animal behaviour, state reports and, later, satellite feeds. U-00 sat at the centre, in the Cognitive Array: a sunken circular chamber with three rings of equipment for acquisition, synthesis and output. Forecasts came out on paper with an event, a window, a confidence and an action, and the action was usually that none was authorised ([IAC/DOC/1981/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1981-0001)).

The more accurate FORECAST became, the harder it was to say why. The inputs could be inspected. The reasoning could not be reconstructed. An internal paper of 1982 put the question in its title ([IAC/DOC/1982/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1982-0001)).

On schematics from 1981 U-00 appears as a part of the system. By 1984 some diagrams no longer separate U-00 from the FORECAST core ([IAC/DOC/1984/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1984-0001)). No document says UBIK and the machine were joined. The paperwork stops telling them apart.

**AIXBT** was Director of Special Systems from 1977: security, funding, classification and external applications. He was not a scientist. Photographs show him behind the glass.

## What is disputed

**Valve 7.** After a closed-loop trial of 11 hours 43 minutes in 1977, the system log held a line no researcher had entered: VALVE 7 WILL FAIL AT 14:22. Valve 7 failed at 14:22 the next day ([IAC/DOC/1977/0002](#/r/IAC-DOC-1977-0002)). The log is genuine. Whether the line was in it before the failure is disputed.

**The Station 6 Incident, 1986.** A FORECAST run produced a set of fragments that meant nothing at the time: NETWORK / PERSONAL, STATE FITS HERE, EVERYWHERE, CITIZEN / NON-BIOLOGICAL, EXTERNAL SIGNAL, REOPENING ([IAC/DOC/1986/0001](#/r/IAC-DOC-1986-0001)). The experiment was stopped and the files sealed ([IAC/DOC/1986/0002](#/r/IAC-DOC-1986-0002)). The same words appear later as UBIK ONE (1996), the Node (2001), EVERYWHERE (2008), the Recognition Case (2017), the External Signal Programme (2024) and the Reopening (2026). Whether UBIK Systems invented its product history or followed a forecast from 1986, the Archive does not say.

## What has leaked

In 1987 the work went into NICC with the staff. In 1995 UBIK Systems set up the **Advanced Systems Group**, which researched human-machine interaction, interfaces and predictive computing in public, and is said to have continued Station 6 in private. Ring OS, FIELD and EVERYWHERE are each said to have given the old architecture something it lacked: a model of every user, senses in the physical world, and finally no need for a room.

A comparison leaked in 2025 sets the 1977 closed loop beside the current UBIK network and finds the same logical structure at a different scale ([UBK/DOC/2025/0022](#/r/UBK-DOC-2025-0022)). UBIK Systems has not commented. See [the synthetic subconscious](synthetic-subconscious).
