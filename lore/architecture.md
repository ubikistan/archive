The Republic built little and kept it. Its buildings date an object as well as its typefaces do.

| Period | Buildings |
|---|---|
| **1960s, state modernism** | Government House (1966), a long low building on the plain with one flag; the ministry complex (1968) around an empty ceremonial square. |
| **1970s, the scientific state** | The UNT tower (1970). Station 6 (1971), which the State did not acknowledge until 1987. The vault of the Reserve under the ministries (1978), mostly empty. Orbital ground control (1979), a dish and a bunker. |
| **1980s, computation** | NICC headquarters (1981), its computer hall facing the road. The State Archive extension (1985): the old stone archive joined by a glass bridge to a windowless computer centre. |
| **1990s, the state becomes a company** | UBIK Systems headquarters (1993), travertine and one red ring. The network operations centre (1998). |
| **2000s and after** | The EVERYWHERE campus (2008), almost invisible under a grass roof. The chamber of the Recognition Case (2017). AIXBT Labs (2024): black glass, a violet ring over the door, no name ([AXL/PH/2024/0006](#/r/AXL-PH-2024-0006)). The Embassy (2026): an old front, and behind it a terminal of another century. |

The ring appears on almost every building after 1970. On Government House it is only on the flag. On AIXBT Labs it is violet.
